FAQ

What is ZEUS SENSI?

ZEUS SENSI is a premium Free Fire sensitivity and HUD generator. It creates optimized settings based on your device brand, phone performance

Is ZEUS SENSI free?

Yes. The generators are free to use with an access PIN distributed by the owner. The PIN list is stored in pins.js.

How do I apply the sensitivity settings?

Open Free Fire > Settings > Sensitivity, then copy each value from your generated result into the matching slot.

How do I apply the HUD code?

Open Free Fire > Settings > HUD > Custom HUD, paste the full code and confirm. The generated code is complete and ready to use.

Why is the app asking for a PIN?

ZEUS SENSI is a premium tool. The owner distributes access PINs; enter a valid PIN to unlock the generators.

Can I change the PIN myself?

Yes. Open pins.js in the same folder as index.html and add, remove or comment out PIN lines. Reload the page and the new PINs are active immediately.
